Scribble Rescue

Draw to save the day!

A picture puzzle game for little kids who can't read yet. Every puzzle is a small picture story — a stuck bunny, a house on fire, a hungry bear. Your child draws something that would help, and their own drawing jumps in and saves the day. Any answer that makes sense counts.

  • 🚫 No ads, no purchases, no accounts
  • 📴 Works offline — nothing leaves the device
  • 🧒 Made for ages 3–7
  • 🗣️ Spoken in English and Vietnamese
