Scribble Rescue – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Developer: Le Ho Di · Contact: hodile90@gmail.com

In short: Scribble Rescue collects no personal information and sends nothing anywhere. It has no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no internet access. Everything happens on your device.

Who this app is for

Scribble Rescue is a picture puzzle game for young children, including children under 13. A child draws something on the screen to solve a little picture story. Because the game is made for children, it is built so that it does not need, and does not collect, any information about them.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, share or sell personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, audio, or usage statistics. It contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

Drawings

When a child finishes a drawing, the app recognises it on the device with a small built-in model. The drawing is not saved and is never transmitted; it is discarded when the puzzle changes.

What stays on the device

The app saves the child's progress (stars earned, puzzles skipped) and settings (sound, music, voice language) in the app's own private storage on the device. This information never leaves the device, is not backed up to the cloud by the app, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Spoken sentences

The app reads short game sentences aloud (such as "Great job!") using the text-to-speech engine that is built into the device. The app chooses on-device voices and only sends the game's own fixed sentences to that engine, never anything about the child. The text-to-speech engine is part of the device's operating system and is covered by its provider's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It cannot access the internet, the camera, the microphone, location or files.

Advertising and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The app has no links to websites or other apps that a child could follow.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the app has no way to do so. This policy is intended to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
